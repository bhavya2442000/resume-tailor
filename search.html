How a search runs

  1. Web search. Your searches run on Greenhouse, Lever and Workday to find companies that are hiring.
  2. Open jobs. Each company's current openings come from its job site, which costs no tokens.
  3. Free filters. Jobs are dropped for the wrong title, too senior, too old, the wrong place, or asking for too many years.
  4. Claude picks. Claude reads the rest with your bank and your own words, and picks the best fits with a reason for each.
